## §0. The synthesis — your two scouting passes are *one tradition*, and the project already mapped most of it

**The single most useful fact.** The Wittgenstein/Austin thread from the last memo and the Cavell/Walton material you loaded as project knowledge are **not two veins — they are one**. Cavell was **Austin's student** and the most ambitious reader of Wittgenstein's *PI* Part II; his **"acknowledgment"** is the developed terminus of exactly the avowal/performative/grammar material the last memo surfaced. Concretely, and now **verified in the KB**:

> Cavell builds his signature distinction — *"Empirical statements that claim truth depend upon evidence; statements that claim truthfulness depend upon our acceptance of them … a true statement is something we know or do not know; a truthful statement is one we must acknowledge or fail or refuse to acknowledge"* [Cavell, *World Viewed* p.157 ✓] — **explicitly as a reading of *PI* Part II** ("my relation to my own words"; "the point at which my knowledge of others depends upon the concepts of truthfulness and interpretation").

That is the *same text* as the passage you flagged at the start (PI p.222: "my own relation to my words is wholly different from other people's"; the first/third-person asymmetry). So the Wittgenstein point that impressed you and Cavell's "acknowledgment" are the **same Wittgensteinian seam, read twice** — once by you, once by Cavell. Your instinct to put the two scouting passes side by side is correct: they converge on one category — *owning/acknowledgment as a non-evidential, non-detective first-person relation*.

**The strategic fact that governs everything below (from `BRIDGE_workings.md`).** The project has *already been here*. The original arc (BRIDGE → `ESSAY_Owning-Up` v1–v4) ran the **heavy thesis**: "first-person access *is* acknowledgment, not knowledge," built on Walton (seeing≠knowing) + Cavell (knowing≠acknowledging). The Stage-6 red-team cut it down — the Walton "demolition" attacked a perceptual reading of Evans **no one holds** (T1 strawman); the "home of knowledge" anchor turned out to be a **pun** (T3); the unification was **pre-empted** by Campbell 2022 / Gertler 2018. The response was the **retreat to v5** — the bounded "receptive conception + idle-wheel" defense that **cites neither Cavell nor Walton** and explicitly brackets the whole acknowledgment story as *"the sequel."*

**So the operating principle for this memo is the inverse of the last one.** Last time (W/Austin), the materials plugged *into v5*. This time, **most of the loaded knowledge is sequel material** — it is the backbone of the heavier paper v5 deliberately set aside. Re-importing it wholesale into v5 would reopen exactly the over-reach the project already (rightly) walked back. Therefore each item below is tagged **[v5-USABLE]** (safe inside the current bounded paper) or **[SEQUEL]** (powerful, but for the heavier successor, not v5).

## PART A — The one place the KB speaks directly to a v5 *open front*: Wright

### A1. Cavell's "acknowledgment is the home of knowledge" as the *gestured* answer to the §5 Wright objection ✓ **[v5-USABLE, but only as a footnote/gesture]**
**Source.** *World Viewed* p.110 ✓: "Painting, being art, is revelation; it is revelation because it is acknowledgment; **being acknowledgment, it is knowledge**, of itself and of its world" — glossed "acknowledgment is the home of knowledge."

**Where it touches v5 — §5, the unbeaten Wright front.** v5 concedes that Wright's deflation (authority is grammar, *changes the subject away from knowledge*) is the residual objection it does **not** defeat, and that adjudicating it "requires the positive theory I have set aside." Cavell's line is the *shape* of the positive answer: a relation can be non-evidential **owning** and *still ground knowledge* rather than discard it — "acknowledgment is the home of knowledge," not its abandonment. That is precisely the wedge against Wright's "mere grammar / changed the subject": owning is not a by-product of the rules that leaves knowledge behind; it is what knowledge of one's own mind *rests on*.

**Stress-test — the red-team already burned this; respect the burn.** Stage-6 **T3**: at p.110 "knowledge" is **modernist painting acknowledging the conditions of painting** — *not* warranted second-order belief. Lifting the slogan into the self-knowledge debate is an equivocation on "knowledge." So this is **not** an in-paper rebuttal of Wright. The honest v5 use is a **single footnote** at §5: "a positive answer to Wright would run through a Cavellian *acknowledgment-grounds-knowledge* claim (truth vs. truthfulness, *World Viewed* p.157); developing it is the task of the sequel." That *names your direction* without smuggling the heavy thesis into the bounded paper — and it strengthens §5 by showing the open front is open *onto something*, not a dead end.

## PART B — The sequel's backbone (where the KB is genuinely load-bearing)

### B1. *Truth vs. truthfulness* (p.157) — the worked name for the "non-receptive element" the idle-wheel test only gestures at ✓ **[SEQUEL]**
**Source.** *World Viewed* p.157 ✓ (quoted in §0).

**Why it matters.** v5's idle-wheel test keeps saying the explanatory work is done by "a non-receptive element — a standing, a commitment, a rule one follows." Cavell supplies the **developed distinction** that element instantiates: *truth* (evidence-dependent, **known**) vs. *truthfulness* (acceptance-dependent, **acknowledged**). This is the **same category** Austin's "giving one's word" (last memo A1) and Moran's assurance (last memo A3) were circling — now with a name and a Wittgensteinian pedigree (it *is* his reading of PI II). For the sequel, "self-ascription is a matter of truthfulness, not truth" is the positive thesis the idle-wheel test clears the ground for.

**Stress-test.** Strong, but **[SEQUEL]** by definition: asserting "truthfulness, not truth" *is* crossing v5's firewall into "non-receptive *and* not-the-receptive-kind-of-knowledge." Also Stage-6 **T4**: don't claim it *derives* authority/Moore/asymmetry; claim it *re-describes and preserves* them. And the truth/truthfulness pair must be defended as more than a relabeling of Bar-On's expression (the **N1** "relabeled expressivism" charge) — its edge is the *acknowledgment/disavowal* failure-structure (self-deception), which expression alone doesn't theorize.

### B2. The **seeing → knowing → acknowledging** ladder — the structural home for "non-receptive" ✓ **[SEQUEL]**
**Source.** `INT_Cavell-Walton_World-Viewed.md` §B.4; Walton 1→2 break (seeing ≠ knowing) internal to Walton; Cavell 2→3 (knowing ≠ acknowledging, "access is not address").

**Why it matters.** It converts v5's binary (receptive vs. non-receptive) into a **three-rung map**: rung 1 *seeing* (perceptual contact, Walton), rung 2 *knowing* (the receptive conception), rung 3 *acknowledging* (owning). The sequel's claim becomes crisp: the receptive conception **freezes self-ascription at rung 2**, and the distinctiveness features (authority, Moore, the person-asymmetry) are **rung-3** phenomena. This is the spine BRIDGE settled on, and it survives the red-team intact (only the *Walton-as-demolition* role was cut, not the ladder).

**Stress-test.** **[SEQUEL]**, and mind the asymmetry caveat (`INT` §B.5 / BRIDGE 3.B DA4): borrow only Walton's *decoupling* (contact ≠ knowledge), **never** his agent-neutral subject-*absence* — the self-case is subject-*present* and asymmetric. Importing Walton's neutrality is what invites the "you modeled self-knowledge on a photograph, i.e. on alienation" objection.

### B3. The **presence/absence axis** — the sequel's diagnostic instrument, and a quiet ally of the v5 idle-wheel ◇/✓ **[SEQUEL, with a v5 resonance]**
**Source.** `TP_Cav.txt` + `BRIDGE` §1.6, §5′.B: Walton's transparency is grounded in the subject's **absence** (belief-independence); reliabilism (Byrne) is the *self*-analog of subject-absence — "a mere **spectator/detector**"; Moran/owning is the **presence** pole.

**Why it matters — and where it lightly touches v5.** The sequel's diagnosis is that the debate's real axis is *subject absence vs. presence*, not *which warrant*. Note the **structural rhyme with v5's idle-wheel**: the "receptive component" v5 shows to be idle for the distinctiveness features is exactly the **absent-subject / detector** pole — reliability tracking with the agent bracketed. So the presence/absence axis is the *metaphysical picture behind* the idle-wheel result. You needn't state it in v5 (it's heavier than the bounded paper wants), but it explains *why* the idle-wheel verdicts come out as they do, and it is ready-made for the sequel.

**Stress-test.** Stage-6 minor: "subject-absence" overstates Byrne (his schema *is* first-personal); soften to **"agent-bracketed-for-warrant."** And "spectator/detector" is Moran's polemic, not neutral description — flag it as such.

### B4. Walton as **existence-proof** (seeing ≠ knowing) — the modal permission slip ✓ **[SEQUEL; faint v5 use]**
**Source.** `KB_Walton` via BRIDGE 2.A / 5′.B: "Perceptual contact … has rather **less to do with acquiring knowledge** … than has sometimes been supposed" [p.269]; flexible-mirror — one sees through a mirror that is "**not a knowledge-producing mechanism**," beliefs via it "may not be knowledge. **But this does not mean that he does not see**" [p.259]; transparency independent of accuracy/reliability [p.273].

**Why it matters.** Walton is the one rigorous, defended case of a "looking-through" relation that is **genuine contact yet not a knowledge-relation** — an *existence-proof in a neighboring domain* that "non-knowledge-but-real-relation" is coherent. For the sequel that licenses taking a non-receptive self-relation seriously; faintly, it could back v5's modest "the question is *open*" (existence-proofs that the receptive picture is optional). But its real job is the sequel's, and **only** as existence-proof + contrast — **not** as a model of self-knowledge (Stage-6 T1: the perceptual reading of the Evans method is a strawman; don't run the "fails every mark" demolition).

## PART C — The gap in the loaded knowledge (the actionable recommendation)

**The Cavell you've loaded is the wrong Cavell for self-knowledge — by register.** `KB_Cavell` is *The World Viewed*, where "acknowledgment" lives in the **medium/aesthetic** key (painting acknowledging its conditions; the camera's outsideness; silence). The KB confirms this: **Austin appears only 3×**, and only as references to Cavell's *other* books; the acknowledgment-of-painting line (p.110) is the one the red-team flagged as a pun for our purposes.

**The directly relevant Cavell is not in the project:**
- **"Knowing and Acknowledging"** (in *Must We Mean What We Say?*, 1969) — *the* text: it argues my relation to another's pain is **acknowledgment, not (failed) knowledge**. That is exactly the structure you would transpose to the **first person**, and it is Cavell-on-Wittgenstein-on-avowal — the same seam as your opening passage.
- **"The Avoidance of Love"** and **"Austin at Criticism"** (same volume) — the Austin/ordinary-language and acknowledgment-of-persons material; the KB cites both but does not contain them.
- ***The Claim of Reason*** (1979), **Part IV** — the worked treatment of other-minds skepticism and acknowledgment.

**Recommendation.** If you take up the sequel, **load "Knowing and Acknowledging" and *Claim of Reason* Part IV** as KBs. *The World Viewed* gives you the *automatism/transparency* spine (which is why it's here, and right for the passage-design track in `INT`) but only the aesthetic face of acknowledgment. The self/other-minds face — the one your essay needs — is in those two texts. (This also de-risks the p.110-pun problem: the "home of knowledge" claim has a non-aesthetic statement in the other-minds material.)

## PART D — SEP KB note + the terminological trap to keep flagged

The `KB_SEP_Self-Knowledge` "**transparency method**" (Evans/Moran: answer "do I believe p?" by looking *outward* to whether p) is a **terminological cousin of Walton's photographic "transparency," not the same notion** (`INT` §D; BRIDGE §3.A "directionality torsion"). The two share only the word. **Keep this firewalled**: Stage-6 **T1** is the lesson — building any argument on the shared term "transparency" attacks a *perceptual* reading of the Evans method that no one holds. Usable as a 〈보기〉/contrast hook on the passage-design track, or as a cautionary aside in the sequel; **never** as load-bearing inference in either paper.
