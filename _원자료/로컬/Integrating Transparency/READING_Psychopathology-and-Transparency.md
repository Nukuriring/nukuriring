# READING MAP — Psychopathology × Transparency

> **What this is.** A web-verified reading list (checked 2026-07-22) for the question 김영진 raised: can a psychopathology like **depersonalization** be tied to *the loss/collapse of transparency*, and are there **kindred disorders** on the same seam? Sorted **by which "transparency" each pathology cracks**, because that sorting is exactly the project's own fault line (경험의 투명성 vs. owning/mineness vs. 실재감 — the senses `BRIDGE_workings.md` and `INT_Cavell-Walton_World-Viewed.md` keep apart).
>
> **How it plugs into the argument.** The load-bearing hope: several of these are cases where **knowing survives while owning/acknowledging fails** (reality-testing intact, mineness gone). If that holds, they are the **self-side existence-proof of the 2→3 break (knowing ≠ acknowledging)** — the joint the Stage-6 red-team called weakest (currently propped only on Cavell's *texts*, "a seam not a proof"). That is the payoff to keep your eye on. Cautions at the bottom (§7) so we don't re-trip the Stage-6 T1 equivocation.
>
> **Access.** ★ = free full text online; ☾ = borrowable (Internet Archive) / paywalled with open preprint.

## 1 — Transparency of *experience* (the medium turns opaque)
*Harman/Tye/Metzinger sense: normally you see through experience to the world; here the "pane of glass" becomes visible.* ⚠ This is the sense that can smuggle back the perceptual-contact picture the project demoted — read it, but see §7.

- **Ciaunica, Charlton & Farmer (2020), "When the Window Cracks: Transparency and the Fractured Self in Depersonalisation,"** *Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences.* ★ — The single most on-the-nose title. Argues DPD alters the **transparency of pre-reflective (embodied) self-consciousness** and the capacity to switch between I-as-subject and I-as-object; first-person reports of an observing-vs-observed split. [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/CIAWTW) · [Springer DOI](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11097-020-09677-z) · [PDF (ResearchGate)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/341489365_When_the_Window_Cracks_Transparency_and_the_Fractured_Self_in_Depersonalisation)
- **Metzinger, *Being No One* (2003)** — background anchor for **phenomenal transparency** (transparency = unavailability of earlier processing stages to introspection). The concept most of this cluster leans on.
- *(secondary)* **"The Opaque Window"** — MA thesis applying Metzinger's transparency to DP phenomenology. Useful survey, not citable weight. [Full text](https://umu.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1666127/FULLTEXT01.pdf)

## 2 — Mineness / ownership (**the project's home turf**)
*The "내 것임" that fails in DP. This is where owning ≠ knowing lives.*

**Experiential & bodily ownership**
- **Billon (2023), "What is it like to lack mineness? Depersonalization as a probe for the scope, nature and role of mineness."** ★ — DP = lack of a phenomenal mark ("mineness") that flags experiences as one's own; uses DP to fix what mineness *is*. [PhilArchive](https://philarchive.org/rec/BILWII)
- **Billon, "Depersonalization and the Sense of Bodily Ownership."** [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/BILDAT-2)
- **Billon, "Mineness First: three challenges to contemporary theories of bodily self-awareness"** — argues mineness is **psychologically primitive**, not reducible to agentive/affective/cognitive dispositions. [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/BILMFT)
- **Billon, "Basic Self-Awareness"** — DP patients' reluctance/inability to use the first-person; his **Cartesian/acquaintance** positive theory. ⚠ *This is where Billon becomes your opponent, not just your evidence — see §6.* [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/BILBS)

**Ownership of *thought* (the bridge from perception/body to *attitudes* — closest to your avowable-attitude scope)**
- **Thought insertion & passivity (schizophrenia)** — the patient reports a thought occurring *in* their mind that is not *theirs*: an ownership/authorship failure at the level of **thinking**, not sensation. This is the cleanest clinical wedge into *owning one's own attitudes*, i.e. first-person authority.
- **Gallagher (2004), "Agency, ownership and alien control in schizophrenia."** ★ — the canonical **sense-of-agency vs. sense-of-ownership** distinction. [PDF (Academia)](https://www.academia.edu/38027654/)
- **Bortolotti & Broome (2009), "A role for ownership and authorship in the analysis of thought insertion,"** *Phen. & Cog. Sciences* — refines toward **ownership + authorship**; directly touches *self-knowledge and rationality in abnormal cognition*. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11097-008-9109-z)
- *(the Campbell–Gallagher exchange on whether agency or ownership is primary is the debate to know here.)*

## 3 — Sense of *reality* / *existence* (Cavellian skepticism, **materialized not wished**)
*This cluster is the tightest fit to your P2′ diagnosis: the demand that the relation "be secured as real/known" = the skeptical reflex. Here patients **live** it.*

- **Billon (2024), "The Psychopathology of Metaphysics: Depersonalization and the Problem of Reality,"** *Metaphilosophy* 55(1): 3–30. ★ **STAR for the skepticism leg.** 19c psychiatrists' "**doute métaphysique**" patients; the clash between an *intuition of unreality* and an *intuition of reality* = the philosophical "problem of reality" lived clinically. This is your skeptical-reflex diagnosis in a psychiatric register. [Free full text (HAL)](https://hal.science/hal-04465558/document) · [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/meta.12666)
- **Billon (2016), "Making Sense of the Cotard Syndrome: Insights from the Study of Depersonalisation,"** *Mind & Language.* ★ — **Cotard** (the delusion of not existing / being dead) as DP's experience *taken at face value*; loss of the "present" and "actual" character of experience. The limit-case of collapsed 실재감/존재감. [PhilArchive](https://philarchive.org/archive/BILMSO) · [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/mila.12110)
- **Gerrans (2023), "A vessel without a pilot: Bodily and affective experience in the Cotard delusion of inexistence,"** *Mind & Language* — rival/complementary account; good for the disanalogy hunt. [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/mila.12441)
- **Ratcliffe, *Feelings of Being: Phenomenology, Psychiatry and the Sense of Reality* (OUP 2008)** ☾ — the philosophical theory of **existential feelings** = the bodily background that *constitutes our sense of reality*; ch. on "feelings of deadness and depersonalization." The framework for treating "reality" as a felt precondition, not an object in experience. [Borrow (Archive)](https://archive.org/details/feelingsofbeingp0000ratc) · [open chapter PDF](https://philosophyofdepression.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/existential-feelings.pdf)

## 4 — Ipseity & hyperreflexivity (**the forced spectator stance = subject-absence, lived**)
*Schizophrenia-spectrum. The tacit becomes an object; the self watches itself. This is Byrne's "detector/spectator" turned into pathology — and, via Sass, the phenomenology side's own bridge to skepticism/solipsism (Cavell-adjacent).*

- **Sass & Parnas (2003), "Schizophrenia, Consciousness, and the Self,"** *Schizophrenia Bulletin* 29(3): 427–444. ★ — the canonical **ipseity-disturbance** model: **hyperreflexivity** (tacit self-aspects thrust into object-awareness) + **diminished self-affection**. Hyperreflexivity ≈ being *forced* off the transparent/tacit stance onto the observer stance. [Free PDF (Oxford)](https://academic.oup.com/schizophreniabulletin/article-pdf/29/3/427/5435825/29-3-427.pdf) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14609238/)
- **Sass, *The Paradoxes of Delusion: Wittgenstein, Schreber, and the Schizophrenic Mind* (Cornell 1994)** ☾ — **the Cavell-adjacent keystone on the clinical side**: schizophrenic experience read through Wittgenstein's critique of **solipsism** — "a consciousness turned in upon itself." The nearest existing weld of *psychopathology ↔ skepticism/solipsism*; your Cavellian move is its analytic-self-knowledge cousin. [Borrow (Archive)](https://archive.org/details/paradoxesofdelus0000sass)
- **Fuchs, "Corporealized and Disembodied Minds"** and **"The Psychopathology of Hyperreflexivity"** ★ — schizophrenic **disembodiment**: the self divorced from a body it experiences "as a Cartesian machine steered by alien force." Loss of tacit bodily transparency. [Hyperreflexivity PDF](https://www.klinikum.uni-heidelberg.de/fileadmin/zpm/psychatrie/fuchs/Hyperreflexivity.pdf) · [Embodiment PDF](https://www.klinikum.uni-heidelberg.de/fileadmin/zpm/psychatrie/fuchs/Embodiment.pdf)

**DP vs. schizophrenia — why DP is the *cleaner* case for you (reality-testing intact isolates the owning-failure):**
- **Sass, Pienkos, Nelson & Medford (2013), "Anomalous self-experience in depersonalization and schizophrenia: A comparative investigation,"** *Consciousness and Cognition* 22(2): 430–441. ★ — maps the overlap (passivity, fading, alienation from thought/body) **and** the differences (self/world confusion + erosion of first-person perspective are *distinctive of schizophrenia*). This is your evidence that DP keeps the "knowing" leg while schizophrenia corrodes it. [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/SASASI) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23454432/)
- **Pienkos & Sass, "'Robbed of my life': The Felt Loss of Familiar and Engaged Presence in Depersonalization/Derealization Disorder."** [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/PIEROM-2)

## 5 — The Cavell / self-knowledge seam (**nearest prior art — read for novelty defense**)
- **Renaudie (2015), "A Pebble at the Bottom of the Water: Sartre and Cavell on the Opacity of Self-Knowledge,"** in *Pre-reflective Consciousness: Sartre and Contemporary Philosophy of Mind* (Routledge). ★ — the closest published weld of **Cavell + self-knowledge + transparency**. Runs **opacity/self-concealment** (via Sartre's translucence/opacity), and concludes self-knowledge is "a way of dealing with one's concealment." *Your* move differs: **owning/answerability** (not concealment), **Walton** as the decoupling lever, and a *psychopathological* existence-proof. Cite as nearest-but-distinct. [Free PDF](https://www.renaudiephilosophy.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Renaudie-Sartre-and-Cavell-on-Self-Knowledge.pdf)

## 6 — Novelty read (for the flywheel)
The neighborhood is **well populated but the specific weld is open**:
- **DP → mineness → reality/skepticism** is thoroughly worked, almost single-handedly by **Billon**. He is therefore your **richest evidence source *and* a live opponent**: his positive theory is a **Cartesian acquaintance/introspection** account of basic self-awareness — i.e. squarely *inside* the privileged-access picture you are trying to dislodge. Use his data on the knowing/owning dissociation; **resist his metaphysics.** (This is a gift for the essay: the leading philosopher of DP defends the very frame you attack, so the disagreement is real and citable, not manufactured — cf. the Walton-fn.4 seam you already prize.)
- **Cavell → self-knowledge** exists (**Renaudie**), but via *opacity/Sartre*, not *owning*, and with **no Walton and no psychopathology**.
- **Schizophrenia → skepticism/solipsism** exists (**Sass**, via *Wittgenstein*), but not DP-specific, not the analytic self-knowledge debate, not *acknowledgment*.
- I found **nothing** welding *depersonalization/thought-insertion → Cavellian acknowledgment → a not-access account of first-person authority*. That is your seam. Crowded border, empty plot. *(Worth a dedicated novelty-scout pass before drafting — this was a first sweep, not exhaustive.)*

## 7 — Caveats to carry (so this doesn't re-trip Stage-6 T1)
1. **Phenomenal mineness ≠ normative owning.** Almost all of this literature means **felt** ownership (a phenomenal mark). Your acknowledgment/owning is **answerability** (a normative stance). If DP subtracts the *feeling* while *answerability* survives, a red-teamer says "you've equivocated on 'owning' just as you once did on 'transparency'." You must **argue the bridge** (feeling grounds/conditions answerability) or **scope the claim**. Don't get this for free.
2. **Scope mismatch.** Most cases here are **body / perception / affect / agency** — precisely the rung-1 *sensation* zone you **bracket** in `BRIDGE` §3.F. **Thought insertion** (Cluster 2) is the one that reaches *thinking/attitudes*; lean on it to reach the avowable-attitude target.
3. **Billon's frame is opposed** (see §6) — his acquaintance/introspection reading is ESF-friendly; cite as data + foil.
4. **The "opaque medium" trap.** Cluster 1's "window/pane" talk tempts the picture that the self-relation is *normally a transparent medium one sees through, now fogged* — the exact perceptual-contact reading your P1′ refuses. Keep it as **phenomenology of the disorder**, not as the model of the healthy relation.
