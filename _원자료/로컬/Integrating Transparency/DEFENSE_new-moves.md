# New arguments & dialectic for the defense paper (middle ambition)

*Invented moves, each stress-tested. Their job: answer the one real threat to the middle-ambition strategy — the empiricist's **burden re-assertion** ("I grant I assume a receptive conception of knowledge; but it is correct, and you owe an alternative; so my objection stands as a demand"). Pure deflection cannot answer this. These moves turn deflection into counter-pressure **without** committing to the full constitutivist metaphysics (which is the sequel, not this paper).*

*Terminology fixed per the evaluation: the contested premise is the **receptive conception of knowledge** — that a second-order self-ascription counts as knowledge only by being controlled-by / answerable-to the attitude as an antecedent, owning-independent fact. The defended views keep "it is knowledge" and reject only "receptive."*

---

## 0. The two explananda-classes (the distinction everything turns on)

- **Class A — reliability/truth:** that our self-ascriptions are, by and large, *true*.
- **Class B — distinctiveness:** the features that make self-knowledge a *distinctive topic* — first-person **authority**, **immediacy/gaplessness**, the **Moore-asymmetry**, and the **error-profile** (we fail by self-deception, not mis-measurement).

We *grant the empiricist Class A* (we never denied self-knowledge is reliable/true). The fight is over Class B. This concession is what keeps the paper at middle ambition and pre-empts the "you're explaining knowledge away" reply.

---

## 1. THE IDLE-WHEEL TEST (the dialectical device — centerpiece)

**The device.** For each Class-B explanandum, ask: *does the **receptive** component of the empiricist's account (reliability / tracking) do the explaining — or must the empiricist smuggle in a **non-receptive** component (a standing, a commitment, a rule one follows, answerability)?* The claim, argued case-by-case in §§2–4: **across Class B, the receptive component is an idle wheel; the explanatory work is done by a non-receptive element the empiricist must borrow.**

**Why it answers the burden re-assertion.** The deflection alone said: "your objection assumes the receptive conception, which I reject" — leaving a symmetric standoff. The Idle-Wheel Test breaks the symmetry: the receptive conception is not merely *one option*, it is *explanatorily idle for the very features that make self-knowledge special*. So "you owe an alternative" rebounds — the non-receptive alternative is *already operating inside the empiricist's own explanations of distinctiveness*, and what the empiricist owes is an account of why, if self-knowledge is receptive, receptivity explains none of the distinctiveness.

**Provenance (cite honestly).** This generalizes **Campbell's Parallelist Dilemma** (which shows reliability is idle for *authority* specifically) into a *systematic test* across all of Class B. Credit Campbell; claim the generalization + the cross-view deployment.

**Risk.** The test is only as strong as the case-by-case verdicts (§§2–4); the empiricist will contest each. And it must stay restricted to Class B — extending "idle" to Class A would falsely deny self-knowledge is reliable/true.

---

## 2. NEW ARGUMENT — Authority-as-Standing (the "wrong kind of deference" argument) — feeds the test on *authority*

- **P1.** First-person authority = the defeasible impropriety of another's overriding one's sincere, present-tense self-ascription of an attitude.
- **P2.** If self-ascription were *receptive*, the proper ground of others' deference would be **evidential**: the avowal reliably indicates the fact, so one defers as to a trusted instrument.
- **P3.** But the deference owed an avowal is not evidential in character. (i) We may *properly* override even a highly reliable instrument given countervailing evidence, and in doing so we do not *wrong* it; overriding a sincere avowal, absent special reason, *wrongs the subject* — it denies her a standing. (ii) The deference is **second-personal** (owed *to her*), not merely owed *to the truth*.
- **C.** First-person authority is a **normative standing**, not an evidential reliability. → Reliability is idle for authority; the non-receptive (answerability/owning) conception predicts it.

**Novelty.** Imports the evidential-vs-second-personal deference distinction (and a wrong-kind-of-reasons flavor) into the self-knowledge debate; a fresh argumentative angle on the Moran/Wright intuition that authority is normative.

**Risk / reply.** The empiricist concedes authority is conventional/normative but says this is compatible with self-knowledge being receptive *knowledge* (knowledge and authority come apart — Wright already separates them). **Reply:** granted — and that *is* the point. If the distinctiveness-mark (authority) is non-receptive, the receptive conception is not earning the distinctiveness; at best it explains Class-A truth. The concession *feeds* the Idle-Wheel Test rather than blunting it.

---

## 3. NEW ARGUMENT — Moore-Triangulation — feeds the test on *the Moore-asymmetry*

- **Data (three cells).** "p, but I don't believe p" (present, 1st-person) is paradoxical. "p, but I didn't believe p" (past, 1st-person) and "p, but S doesn't believe p" (present, 3rd-person) are *not*.
- **P1.** A *receptive* (detection) relation is **symmetric across time and person**: detecting my present belief is the same *kind* of act as detecting my past belief or another's belief. So it predicts *no special present-1st-person incoherence* — at most a possibly-false conjunction, exactly as in the other two cells.
- **P2.** The incoherence is *specifically* present-1st-person.
- **C.** Present-1st-person self-ascription is **not a detection**; the incoherence is one of **commitment** — asserting *p* just is undertaking the commitment whose disavowal the second conjunct attempts. Non-receptive.

**Idle-wheel rider.** The empiricist (Byrne) is *not silent* on Moore — but his account runs through the **schema/rule** "p ∴ I believe p," which is a *committal, rule-following* element, not a tracking element. So even the empiricist's Moore-account is powered by a non-receptive component → reliability idle.

**Novelty.** The time×person *triangulation*, used as an argument that present-1st-person self-ascription is non-receptive (receptivity predicts symmetry; the data show asymmetry). A sharp, fairly novel deployment of a worked-over phenomenon.

**Risk / care.** Must *not* claim the empiricist has no Moore-account (false). The claim is that the operative part of any adequate Moore-account is non-receptive (commitment/rule-following), which is what the rider establishes.

---

## 4. NEW ARGUMENT — Gettier-Immunity (supporting; FLAGGED) — feeds the test on *security*

- **P1.** Receptive knowledge is *Gettierizable*: because belief and fact are independent, they can be linked by luck (justified true belief that is not knowledge).
- **P2.** There appear to be **no genuine Gettier cases** for present-tense self-ascription of one's avowable, occurrent attitudes (one cannot *luckily-truly* self-ascribe an intention). (Distinguish from *error* cases like Kate-and-the-therapist, which are false self-ascriptions, not Gettiered true ones.)
- **C.** The relation is not a contingent belief–fact link → non-receptive.

**Empiricist reply & our counter.** Byrne: *safety* explains the Gettier-immunity. Counter: the *reason* present-tense self-ascription is safe is that the fact is *not independent* of the ascription — so safety here is *explained by* non-receptivity, and reliability is again parasitic.

**FLAG — this is where the heavier commitment peeks through.** The counter ("the fact is not independent of the ascription") *is* the constitutivist (non-independence) claim we are otherwise only gesturing at. So Gettier-Immunity pulls past middle ambition. **Recommendation:** present it as *suggestive/supporting* and as the visible seam toward the sequel (the full constitutivist paper), not as a load-bearing move here. Possibly hold in reserve.

---

## 5. NEEDLE — Tu-Quoque / Self-Application (non-probative opener)

The empiricist's *own theoretical avowals* — "I believe empiricism is true," her report of her own credence in the doxastic schema — are, in their phenomenology and their normative profile, exercises of *answerable commitment*, not deliverances of inner detection. The receptive conception sits awkwardly with the self-knowledge the theorist *deploys in the act of asserting it*.

**Use.** A motivational opener / source of rhetorical pressure — **not** load-bearing. The empiricist has a standing reply (immediacy = the effortlessness of a reliable process). Flag as non-probative; use to *frame*, not to *prove*.

---

## 6. The assembled dialectical spine (new architecture for v5)

1. **Unification (backbone).** The primary objection to agentialism (change-the-topic / control-by-not-over) and the primary objection to expressivism (groundless ⇒ no achievement) are *one objection*: the receptive conception applied to a non-receptive relation. → The two views stand or fall together; the objections beg the question. *[Deflection.]*
2. **Empiricist comeback.** *Burden re-assertion*: "the receptive conception is correct; you owe an alternative."
3. **The turn — Idle-Wheel Test.** For Class B (authority, immediacy, Moore-asymmetry, error-profile), the receptive component is explanatorily idle; the work is done by smuggled non-receptive elements. Powered by **Authority-as-Standing** (§2) and **Moore-Triangulation** (§3); **Gettier-Immunity** (§4) in reserve. → The burden reverses: the empiricist owes an account of receptivity's idleness for distinctiveness.
4. **Bounded conclusion (middle ambition).** We have *not* proven constitutivism. We have shown that the receptive conception is explanatorily idle for the features that make self-knowledge distinctive — so the standard objections do not merely beg the question, they deploy an *explanatorily idle* premise. That is a strong deflection *plus* a positive, non-metaphysically-committal gesture toward the non-receptive conception. The point at which the fuller, heavier commitment would take over (the constitutivist ontology) is marked precisely — it is where the Gettier argument's counter lives — and flagged as the sequel.

---

## 7. Honest scorecard

- **Idle-Wheel Test:** the keystone; genuinely answers the burden re-assertion; novel as a *systematic* device (seeded by Campbell). Strength = the §2–4 cases.
- **Authority-as-Standing:** strongest new argument; fresh framing; the empiricist's best reply *feeds* the test. **Build it.**
- **Moore-Triangulation:** cleanest; uses a phenomenon the empiricist must also explain; the rider keeps it honest. **Build it.**
- **Gettier-Immunity:** seductive but leans constitutivist; **hold in reserve / use only to mark the seam to the sequel.**
- **Tu-Quoque:** rhetoric, not proof; **opener only.**
- **What none of these do:** settle whether self-knowledge is *really* non-receptive (the constitutivism-vs-Humeanism question). That is deliberately left open — the paper's honesty and its modest size depend on leaving it open.
