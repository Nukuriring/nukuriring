## STAGE 1 — Frame & target lock

### 1.1 The target, named: the **Epistemic-Security Frame (ESF)**
The frame we are problematizing is the assumption that *what is distinctive and philosophically at stake in first-person access is its standing as **knowledge** with superior epistemic credentials.* Its three sub-commitments:
1. **Explanatory target is a knowledge-relation** — the thing to be explained is *self-knowledge*, true/warranted second-order representation.
2. **Success = securing warrant** — a good account shows self-ascriptions are especially secure (more certain/safe/authoritative than ordinary empirical knowledge).
3. **A method/ground must deliver that warrant** — introspection, reliability, normative entitlement, etc., are proposed precisely *as warrant-suppliers*.

The intervention's target is this shared *aim*, not any single account's choice of ground.

### 1.2 The frame is **shared**, not a camp-thesis
From `KB_SEP_Self-Knowledge.md`:
- **§1 (four marks).** All four — epistemic security, special method, agency, first-person authority — individuate self-knowledge by its *epistemic* specialness. Tellingly, even the agency mark is enlisted epistemically: "most who hold (3) also claim the agential relation *grounds* a special **epistemic** relation."
- **§3.4 Empiricist transparency** (Byrne, Dretske, Fernández). Credit = **reliability/safety**, an externalist *warrant*. Pure epistemic telos.
- **§3.7 Agentialist transparency** (Moran). Transparency is licensed by *normative responsibility*, but the cash-value is still that "its self-ascriptions are **knowledge**." The ground is relocated; the knowledge-aim is retained.
- **§3.8 Expressivism.** Even the escape route stays inside: live versions (Bar-On, Campbell) fight to show avowals *express self-**knowledge*** (Bar-On: "genuine and privileged self-knowledge"; Campbell grounds first-person **privilege**). The one position that exits the ESF — **Simple Expressivism** (avowals neither true nor false, express *no knowledge*) — is treated by the field as the option to *avoid*.

**Upshot.** Across otherwise opposed camps, *abandoning the knowledge-claim is treated as a reductio.* That reflex — "if it isn't knowledge, we've lost the phenomenon" — **is** the ESF. Naming it as a frame (a droppable presupposition) rather than a result is the first move of the intervention.

### 1.3 The frame is **optional**: Walton as an existence-proof
Walton constructs, in a neighboring domain, a rigorous and defended notion of **transparency = perceptual contact** that is *explicitly severed from knowledge*:
- "Perceptual contact with things has rather **less to do with acquiring knowledge** about them than has sometimes been supposed." [Walton p. 269/271]
- **Flexible-mirror case** [p. 259]: one *sees through* a wildly unreliable mirror that is "**not a knowledge-producing mechanism**"; beliefs formed via it "may not be **knowledge**. **But this does not mean that he does not see** the reflected things."
- Transparency is independent of accuracy, detail, information, and reliability [p. 273].

So a "looking-through / transparency" relation **can be genuine world-contact and yet not a knowledge-relation.** This is the conceptual permission slip: the ESF's tacit identification of *transparency* with *an epistemic warrant-method* is a substantive, optional assumption — Walton instantiates the alternative. (N.B. this is an *existence proof in another domain*; whether the analogy transfers to mental states is Stage 3's burden, not assumed here.)

### 1.4 Why the field can't drop it on its own: the **Cavellian diagnosis**
Cavell supplies the explanation of the ESF's grip. The demand that our relation to the world *be* knowledge — that contact be *secured* as certainty/reliability — is, for Cavell, the **skeptical impulse** itself; skepticism's "truth" is that the relation we actually stand in is **acknowledgment**, not knowing (`KB_Cavell` Ch. 19, "The Acknowledgment of Silence"; to be grounded textually in Stage 2). And from `TP_Cav.txt`: the photographic *automatism* (belief-independence) returns the world but with the **subject absent** — "a world I am screened from" — i.e., skepticism *materialized* in a medium.

**Our reflexive transposition (flagged as a constructed step, not Cavell's own application).** Cavell deploys acknowledgment chiefly against *external-world* and *other-minds* skepticism. Turning it on the *self* — "the ESF's demand that the first-person relation be *knowledge* is itself a skeptical reflex, and the truth of 'self-knowledge' may be **self-acknowledgment**" — is *our* move, to be argued, not borrowed ready-made. This is the hinge to the avowal/acknowledgment pole, now motivated independently of expressivism's own resources.

### 1.5 Guardrail — the explananda a replacement must **not** lose
Dropping the knowledge-telos risks "changing the subject." Whatever replaces the ESF must still re-derive the data that made self-knowledge look special — from *acknowledgment/contact*, not by fiat:
- **Asymmetry** — I stand to my own states differently than to others', and than others do to mine.
- **First-person authority** — the presumption of truth; the impropriety of answering "I believe it's raining" with "no, you don't."
- **Moore-paradox** — the distinctive *irrationality* of "p, but I don't believe p."
- *(candidate fourth)* the **groundless immediacy** of avowal (no evidence, no intervening judgment).

The Expressivist pole already addresses authority (avowal) and the Moore-link; so the resources for re-derivation exist. This list is the acceptance-test for any positive proposal.

### 1.6 The fault line revealed (preview of the lesson)
Walton + Cavell suggest the debate's *real* axis is not **[reliable warrant vs. normative warrant]** but **[the subject's *absence* vs. *presence*]** in the transparency relation:
- Walton's photographic transparency is grounded in the subject's **absence** (belief-independence / automatism) → which Cavell reads as **skepticism** (the world-without-me, alienation).
- **Byrne's** reliabilist self-knowledge is the *self*-analog of subject-**absence**: the subject as a site through which a reliable transition runs — exactly the agentialist charge of "a mere **spectator** (or detector) of a cognitive show."
- **Moran / Boyle / avowal** sit on the **presence** side.

The ESF is *blind to this axis* because it abstracts from the subject's mode of involvement and asks only "is it warranted?" **Provisional intervention thesis (to be earned, not assumed):** relocate the question onto the presence/absence axis; self-knowledge belongs on the **acknowledgment-presence** side — which is why neither reliabilism (absence) nor a knowledge-aiming entitlement (Moran's residual ESF) was ever going to be the whole story.

### 1.7 Reuse from the sibling INT track — the seeing → knowing → acknowledging ladder
A parallel doc in this folder, `INT_Cavell-Walton_World-Viewed.md`, already builds the Walton×Cavell(×Bazin) integration on the **photography** side (for passage design). Two of its results are load-bearing for *this* (self-knowledge) track and are adopted here:

- **The evaluative ladder (INT §B.4): SEEING → KNOWING → ACKNOWLEDGING.**
  - Rung 1, **SEEING** = Walton's transparency (perceptual contact).
  - The **1→2 break (SEEING ≠ KNOWING)** is *internal to Walton* (flexible mirror; "less to do with acquiring knowledge").
  - The **2→3 step (KNOWING ≠ ACKNOWLEDGING)** is Cavell's ("access is not address"; Ch. 19).
  Transposed: **the ESF freezes the first-person relation at rung 2 (KNOWING); the contribution argues it belongs at rung 3 (ACKNOWLEDGING), and Walton's 1→2 break is precisely what makes a *non-knowledge* first-person relation conceptually available.** This ladder, not a bare "absence/presence" slogan, is the spine.

- **The asymmetry caveat (INT §B.5) — and why it *protects* the argument.** Walton's subject-absence is *agent-neutral / symmetric / epistemic*; Cavell's is *existential / asymmetric* (the subject's exclusion is constitutive; the *wish* is the point). Consequence for the transposition: **Walton's job is confined to the 1→2 break (contact can be decoupled from knowledge); we do NOT import his agent-neutral subject-absence into the self-case.** The positive model for self-knowledge is Cavell's *asymmetric, first-personal* acknowledgment (subject-present). This blocks in advance the strongest objection — "modeling self-knowledge on photographs imports alienation/spectator-absence" — because we borrow only Walton's *decoupling*, never his *neutrality*.

**Revised preview thesis (to be earned in Stages 3–5):** *first-person "self-knowledge" is mis-located at rung 2 (knowing) and is properly a rung-3 relation — self-**acknowledgment**. Walton licenses the move off rung 2 by proving contact ≠ knowledge; Cavell supplies rung 3; the built-in asymmetry of the first-person case (INT §B.5) makes the Cavellian — not the Waltonian — reading the apt positive model. The empiricist/reliabilist camp (Byrne) is then diagnosable as a rung-2 reading that drifts toward Walton's agent-neutral absence (the "spectator" charge), and Moran's agentialism as a rung-2 reading straining toward rung 3 without the vocabulary to land there.*

## STAGE 2 — The two probes

A "probe" distills one source to the *mechanism we carry into the self-knowledge debate*, plus the explicit *limits* (where it stops being Walton/Cavell and becomes our construction). Per the confirmed spine: **Walton supplies the 1→2 break (seeing ≠ knowing); Cavell supplies the 2→3 step (knowing ≠ acknowledging).** Anchors are cited for Stage 6 re-verification.

### 2.A — WALTON probe: contact can be decoupled from knowledge (rung 1→2)
**Transferable kernel (borrowed):**
- **(W1) Contact ≠ knowledge.** A relation can be genuine, direct *contact* with its object yet not a knowledge/warrant/reliability relation. Anchors: "Perceptual contact … has rather **less to do with acquiring knowledge** … than has sometimes been supposed" [p.269]; flexible-mirror — one sees through a mirror that is "**not a knowledge-producing mechanism**," beliefs via it "may not be knowledge. **But this does not mean that he does not see**" [p.259]; transparency is independent of accuracy/detail/information/reliability [p.273]; seeing ≠ learning — seeing the dead through old photos [p.251].
- **(W2) The mark of the relation is *structural*, not epistemic.** What makes a process *perceptual* is not warrant but **structural correspondence** — it counts as perceptual only if its *pattern of likely confusions mirrors real similarity* among the objects [pp.270–271]. Belief-independence is *necessary but not sufficient* (description-machine objection); structural correspondence is the further condition.

**Limit — NOT borrowed (per INT §B.5):**
- **(W-x1) Subject-absence / agent-neutrality.** In Walton the relation is grounded in the subject's *absence* (belief-independence: no believing mind in the chain) and is *agent-neutral / symmetric*. We **refuse** this for the self-case (constitutively subject-*present*, asymmetric). We take only the *decoupling* (W1) and the *structural-mark form* (W2) — never the absence that grounds them in Walton.
- **(W-x2) The literal belief-independence criterion** (counterfactual dependence holding the *maker's* beliefs fixed) does not transfer — there is no maker distinct from the subject in the self-case; only its *form* (mark = structural, not warrant) transfers.

**Debt → Stage 3 (W-D1).** If we keep (W2), *what is the first-person analog of structural correspondence?* Either find one (a structural feature of self-relation playing the role that confusion-mirrors-similarity plays for perception) **or** argue the first-person contact-mark is something else (the avowal/expression relation, or Cavellian *acknowledgment* itself). This is the probe's main liability.

### 2.B — CAVELL probe: knowing is not acknowledging (rung 2→3)
*Now textually grounded in `KB_Cavell` Ch.19 + master glossary (read this session).*
- **(C1) The knowing-wish *is* the skeptical wish.** Skepticism is realized in the wish to **"view the world unseen"** — to be satisfied of the world's reality *while absent from it*: "viewing unseen … exactly the price of skepticism" [≈p.40/101]; film "realizes skepticism," the senses "satisfied of reality while reality does not exist, because viewing it is all it takes" ["More" ≈pp.188–189]; the screen "screens me from the world it holds … and screens that world from me" [p.24]; "present to me while I am not present to it … a world past" [p.23]. **Gloss:** the skeptical posture wants the world *secured (known)* without the exposure of being *present/answerable* — i.e., **to know without acknowledging.**
- **(C2) Acknowledgment is the rung-3 category — and the *home* of knowledge, not its abandonment.** Cavell's cleanest formulation, and it is about *statements/avowals*: **"a true statement is something we know or do not know; a truthful statement is one we must *acknowledge* or fail or refuse to acknowledge"** [p.157]. Acknowledgment is "not *reference* but an *act* of the self," called **"the home of knowledge"** [glossary; "the arrival of an understanding is a question of acknowledgment" p.107; "being acknowledgment, it is knowledge, of itself and of its world" p.110]. → Acknowledgment does **not** discard knowledge; it *grounds* it. (Directly answers the "you abandoned the knowledge-claim" objection — guardrail §1.5.)
- **(C3) Cavell *himself* casts self-knowledge as acknowledgment-laden self-revelation — the transposition is textually seeded, not only ours.** Ch.19 close [p.160]: **"The knowledge of the self as it is always takes place in the betrayal of the self as it was. That is the form of self-revelation, until the self is wholly won"**; "love is always the betrayal of love, if it is honest"; the *Hiroshima mon amour* mirror-confession registers "**the need for acknowledgment**, because what she has betrayed is her love for him, which is not dead." For Cavell, self-knowledge is *honesty / owning / acknowledgment* (self-revelation through betraying the former self) — **not** epistemic access to a standing inner fact.

**Limit — NOT borrowed / must construct:**
- **(C-x1)** Medium-specific apparatus (screen, automatism-of-medium, color, silence, film-ontology) stays on the photography track (INT).
- **(C-x2)** Cavell does not *systematically* theorize the self-knowledge debate; p.160 is a seam, not a worked epistemology. Turning truth/truthfulness into a *position on first-person authority + Moore-paradox* is **our** reconstruction (Cavell's prose is nonlinear — INT caution). Debt = **C-D1**.

### 2.C — Novelty seam (asset + liability): acknowledgment vs. the SEP §3.8 avowal pole
The debate already has an expression/avowal pole (Bar-On, Finkelstein, Campbell) and an alienation/ownership theme (Moran). Cavellian *acknowledgment* is their kin → the red-team's sharpest charge will be **"you've just relabeled expressivism."** Reply-in-waiting: acknowledgment adds (i) the **diagnosis** (C1) of *why* the field clings to the knowing-frame — it is the skeptical reflex; and (ii) the **truth/truthfulness re-categorization** (C2) that makes self-ascription a matter of *owning/accepting* (truthfulness) rather than *detecting* (truth), and *grounds* rather than discards knowledge. Whether (i)+(ii) are *enough* novelty over Bar-On/Campbell is the Stage 5/6 burden. Flag = **N1**.

## STAGE 3 — Juxtaposition → analogy ledger → lesson
*Per steer, the truth/truthfulness naming is held **neutral**; the ledger decides whether it is earned.*

### 3.A The mapping under audit — and the torsion
The terminological convergence assigns roles:

| role | Walton (photographic) | Evans "transparency method" (self-knowledge) |
|---|---|---|
| who | viewer | subject |
| transparent medium ("looked through") | the photograph | the mental state (belief) |
| what is *reached* | the object / world | the world (whether-*p*) |
| what is *known* as a result | the object / world | the **mental state** (that I believe *p*) |

The bottom two rows expose the **directionality torsion**: for Walton the *direction of attention* (outward, through the photo) and the *target of knowledge* (the world) **coincide**; in the self-case they **cross** — attention goes outward (to whether-*p*), but the knowledge is of the *state*. Walton's transparency *effaces* the medium (you never learn about the photo); the Evans method needs you to *learn about the very medium you look past.* The shared word "transparency" papers over a difference in the relation itself.

### 3.B The analogy ledger (feature-by-feature)

| # | Walton feature | Transfers? | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | contact decoupled from knowledge (a coherent shape) | **modal license only** | we may take a *non-knowledge* self-relation seriously — licenses, doesn't prove |
| W2 | the mark is *structural*, not warrant | **form yes, content no** | the structure is acknowledgment-failure, not similarity-confusion (3.C) |
| DA1 | attention-direction = knowledge-target (both outward) | **breaks (torsion)** | the "transparency method" is *not* Walton-transparency; the perceptual metaphor is unearned (L-neg) |

### 3.C Paying W-D1 — the first-person analog of "structural correspondence"
Walton: a process is perceptual only if its *pattern of likely confusions mirrors real similarity* (you confuse an 85-ft tree with an 84-ft one, not with the word "tree"). Ask the parallel of self-ascription: *what is one liable to get wrong, and how is that liability structured?* The characteristic first-person failures are **not misidentifications by similarity** but **self-deception, evasion, denial — failures of owning.** So the slot Walton's *similarity-structure* fills is filled, in the self-case, by an **acknowledgment/disavowal structure**: W2's *form* transfers (the mark is structural, not a warrant), its *content* is moral-agential, not perceptual. (This is the data the debate already files under §3.3 self-interpretation, §3.7 alienation, and recalcitrant/akratic/wishful attitudes "that survive deliberation" — the acknowledgment frame *organizes* it.)

### 3.D Walton's role, stated honestly (a demotion that is a strength)
Walton is **not** the model of self-knowledge (DA1–DA2 forbid it). His two real jobs:
1. **Existence-proof (modal premise):** "a looking-through relation can be genuine yet not a knowledge-relation" is coherent and instantiated — so a self-relation that is not knowledge is no contradiction. This licenses rung-3 against the privileged-access reflex.
2. **Measuring-rod:** Walton specifies what perceptual transparency-as-contact *requires* (belief-independence, structural correspondence, medium-effacement, aligned directionality, agent-neutrality). Held against the rod, the self-knowledge "transparency method" fails **every** requirement → the perceptual/access metaphor at the heart of the privileged-access picture is **borrowed prestige, not earned contact.**

The positive identification (acknowledgment) is **Cavell's** job, not Walton's. That is not a weakness of the detour — it is its point: *Walton makes the metaphor measurable, hence falsifiable.*

### 3.E The lesson (held neutral on naming)
- **L-neg (against the perceptual/access reading).** Measured against a rigorous theory of perceptual transparency, the self-knowledge "transparency method" — and the broader privileged-*access* picture — does **not** denote a contact- or access-relation (DA1, DA2, W-D1). Its perceptual metaphor is unearned.
- **L-pos (toward acknowledgment).** The very features that break the perceptual model — the torsion, possible constitution of the state by the ascription, ineliminable subject-presence, a self-*error* structure of self-deception — are **native** to an *acknowledgment / owning* relation (rung 3): no detection, hence no torsion; no independent object required; subject present by definition; failure = disavowal. Cavell supplies the category and seeds it for the self-case (p.160).
- **Neutrality note (per steer):** the ledger *points toward* naming rung-3 **truthfulness/owning** ("no detection / no torsion" is just what "truthful (accepted) vs true (known)" encodes) — but the lesson does **not rest** on that naming; the decision is deferred.

### 3.F Scope sharpening (cashing "narrow as we go")
DA3 sharpens the live target from "self-knowledge" to **avowable, reasons-sensitive attitudes** (belief, intention — the Moran/avowal zone), where the privileged-access picture is most contested and acknowledgment bites hardest. *Sensations* (pain) are bracketed (plausibly genuine rung-1 acquaintance; over-extending acknowledgment to pain is a known failure mode). This is also exactly where §3.4 (Byrne) and §3.7 (Moran) actually fight.

The acknowledgment reading **dissolves** the torsion by denying any transition/detection occurs.

### Candidate C — *Global:* the privileged-access picture as a skeptical artifact (§1.1–1.2 vs §2)
- **Locus.** The framing itself (four marks, security, special method) *and* its skeptical opponents (§2 Ryle, Schwitzgebel, Carruthers).

## STAGE 5 — The master argument (Site B) + defense

**Thesis (B).** The empiricist-vs-agentialist dispute over the "transparency method" is not a disagreement about *what warrants* a method of self-knowledge; it is an artifact of forcing a **rung-3 phenomenon (acknowledgment / owning) into a rung-2 frame (knowledge / access)**. Byrne and Moran are not rival warrant-theories of one relation but two *distortions* of acknowledgment — Byrne drops the agent to secure reliability (subject-absence); Moran keeps the agent but mis-cashes owning as knowledge (residual access-telos). First-person access to avowable, reasons-sensitive attitudes is **acknowledgment** (truthful owning) — which is why the warrant-debate was interminable.

### 5.A The master argument (premise form)
- **P1 — measuring-rod (L-neg).** Against a rigorous account of perceptual transparency (Walton), the "transparency method" fails every constitutive mark — decisively the *directionality torsion* (DA1). ∴ it is **not** a perceptual contact/access relation; its perceptual metaphor is unearned.
- **P2 — the warrant-framing is perceptual residue.** Both camps inherit from that metaphor the picture of the method as a *route to knowing* one's belief, whose credentials are then disputed (the *warrant* question). If P1 holds, there is no such route. ∴ the warrant question is ill-posed — no transition to warrant.
- **P3 — absence/presence diagnosis.** Byrne's reliabilism makes the subject a *site* a safe transition runs through, the agent's grasp irrelevant (Boyle's "won't seem reasonable") = the self-analog of Walton's **subject-absence** (the spectator/detector). Moran insists on the subject as *present* agent (owning/answerability) = a **presence**-structure reaching for rung 3 — but retains the knowledge-telos, so must over-intellectualize owning into a warranted route (O'Brien's "too demanding").
- **P4 — the rung re-description + Cavell's category.** The variable separating Byrne and Moran is **which rung**, not which warrant. Cavell's *truthfulness vs truth* ("a truthful statement is one we must acknowledge"; acknowledgment = "the home of knowledge") supplies the rung-3 category that **completes** Moran: first-person access to avowable attitudes is **acknowledgment** — owning, not a warranted route.
- **C — conclusion.** ∴ The standoff dissolves: Byrne describes a reliable *correlation* (a detector, agent bracketed) — true but not the self-*authoritative* phenomenon; Moran describes *owning* but mis-cashes it as knowledge. The phenomenon is acknowledgment; the warrant-debate failed because it mis-categorized a rung-3 relation as rung-2.

### 5.B Re-deriving the explananda (acceptance test §1.5)
- **Asymmetry** = answerability/authorship: I *own* my attitudes (am answerable for them); others can only *attribute* them. (Moran's real insight, freed from the knowledge-telos.)
- **First-person authority** = the standing of the one who *owns/avows*; "no, you don't" refuses my owning — a category-error about answerability, not a rival measurement (Cavell p.157 directly).
- **Moore-paradox** = a *disavowal of what one is in the act of asserting*: asserting *p* is owning the commitment to *p*, so "*p* but I don't believe *p*" is incoherent at the level of *owning*, not of evidence (more direct than the reliabilist's contingent-correlation route).
- **Avowal's groundless immediacy** = native: owning is not evidence-based and has no intervening judgment — that is what owning *is* (no detection → no ground required).

### 5.C Realignment of the eight SEP accounts (rung lens)
- Acquaintance, Inner Sense → rung-1/2 contact-models (apt perhaps for *sensations*, not avowable attitudes).
- Self-Interpretation, Empiricist Transparency → rung-2 *with subject-absence* (inference/reliability — the detector pole; Byrne the purest case).
- Reasons, Rationalist → rung-2 internalizing the transition (judgment/rationality) — nearer owning, still knowledge-framed.
- **Agentialist** (Moran, Boyle, Bilgrami, McGeer, Coliva) → rung-2 *straining toward rung-3* (owning, commitment, performative) — the closest; the account the thesis **completes**.
- **Expressivist** (Bar-On, Campbell) → rung-3 in *content* (avowal, groundless) but under-theorized as to *why* (Campbell's open problem) — the thesis **supplies the why**.
→ The contribution sits at the **convergence of Agentialism (owning) and Expressivism (avowal)**, named/grounded by Cavellian acknowledgment, with Walton clearing the perceptual metaphor. *That convergence is the novelty.*

### 5.D Objections & replies
- **O1 — "Relabeled expressivism" (N1).** (a) Expressivism asserts avowals express self-knowledge but leaves the epistemology a bare "presumption of truth" — it doesn't explain *why* privilege holds (Campbell's own open problem); acknowledgment supplies it (owning/answerability; failure = self-deception, not unreliability). (b) The thesis is not a theory of avowals (a speech-act class) but a *re-diagnosis of the transparency debate* — explaining why Byrne and Moran are distortions of one phenomenon, which expressivism never did. (c) It **unifies** the agentialist-owning and expressivist-avowal poles — synthesis, not relabel.
- **O2 — "Residual access-telos in Moran doesn't stick."** The O'Brien lever: Moran needs the subject to *recognize her reasons for p as evidence that she believes p* — a knowledge-move. If owning were the whole story, no such evidential recognition is needed (you needn't have evidence that you believe *p* in order to *own* believing *p*). The over-demandingness is *produced* by the retained knowledge-telos → proof it does real (bad) work.
- **O3 — "You abandoned knowledge / changed the subject."** "Acknowledgment is the home of knowledge" (p.110): it *grounds* self-knowledge, doesn't discard it. True second-order beliefs persist downstream; the claim concerns their *ground* and the *source of authority*. All four explananda preserved (5.B) → re-grounded, not changed.
- **O4 — Byrne's externalist shrug ("I claimed only safety").** Conceded — and it concedes the point: reliability brackets the agent, so it describes a *detector* (accuracy without authority), not the first-person-authoritative phenomenon. True of *tracking*, not *owning*.
- **O5 — Over-dramatization (DA4′).** Distinguish the *form* (owning/answerability — present in every case, even trivial Moore-pairs) from the *intensity* (existential limit cases — Hiroshima — where the form is most visible). The form does the work; the drama illustrates.
- **O6 — Scope.** Conceded and principled (3.F): sensations plausibly stay rung-1 acquaintance; the thesis targets avowable/reasons-sensitive attitudes (the contested Byrne-vs-Moran zone). A hybrid (acquaintance + acknowledgment) is consistent — SEP allows hybrids.

### 5′.A The shared, non-equivocal target — the knowledge-telos (ESF)

### 5′.B The master argument (premise form)
- **P1′ — Walton: existence-proof + contrast (no demolition).** Walton exhibits a "looking-through" relation that is *genuine contact yet not knowledge* (flexible mirror; "contact … has less to do with knowledge … than supposed"). Two modest yields: **(a) existence-proof** — a first-personal/looking-through relation that is *not* a knowledge-relation is coherent and instantiated, so the ESF's "it must be knowledge" is *optional*, not necessary; **(b) contrast/triangulation** — Walton's relation is *perceptual contact*, which the self-relation is **not** (you don't perceive your belief; Evans's "looking through" is a metaphor for *not* attending to the state). So the self-relation is neither Waltonian contact (rung 1) nor — given P2′–P3′ — knowledge-access (rung 2); Walton makes visible that it is a *third* thing. *(This claims no perceptual reading of Evans and runs no "fails every mark" demolition — the Stage-6 T1 strawman is removed.)*
- **P2′ — Cavell: the etiological diagnosis (the surviving novelty).** Why does the field treat "not-knowledge" as a reductio? Because the demand that a relation *be* knowledge — that contact be *secured* as warranted certainty — is the **skeptical reflex** (Cavell); its disappointment is skepticism, its alternative is *acknowledgment* (truth vs **truthfulness**, p.157 — verified). *Transposing to the self is our move* (Cavell deploys acknowledgment against *other-minds*/external-world skepticism; p.160 is a resonant seam, not proof): the demand that the **first-person** relation be knowledge is the same reflex; its alternative is **self-acknowledgment**.

- **C′ — Contribution (honestly scoped).** (1) *Core/headline:* the ESF is **optional** (P1′) and **diagnosable as a skeptical reflex** (P2′) — a new **diagnostic instrument** trained on the whole debate, reached by a route (Walton + Cavell) no one has taken.
