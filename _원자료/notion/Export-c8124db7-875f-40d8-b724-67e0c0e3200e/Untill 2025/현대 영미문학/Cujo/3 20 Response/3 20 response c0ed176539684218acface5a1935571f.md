In this response, I wish to point out that Donna’s fear of Cujo is not a result of mere survival instinct. Her fear, on the one hand, is caused by the physical threat posed by Cujo, on the other hand, it is caused by the unintelligibility of Cujo.

When Donna hears a "low thick growling"(p.203), she tries to pinpoint where the growling sound comes from. She fails to identify the source of the sound. Then, she suddenly gets "terrified, not by the sound itself but by its seeming directionlessness"(p.204). It seems clear that Donna’s fear is not about the horrifying "low thick growling". Her fear derives from the sheer feeling of disorientation.

Donna discovers Cujo and realizes that somehow Cujo is not the same Cujo she knew before. In a state of heightened alertness, she recalls that "It did not growl before"(p.204). She senses that Cujo has changed in a fundamental way but can not tell how exactly it has changed. Cujo is both Cujo and not Cujo, according to her.Cujo becomes an uncanny figure to Donna. At this point, Cujo starts to be referred to as "the dog". Its stare paralyzes Donna. Donna loses control of her own body. She lost the contact "between her brain and her legs", and her hands became "stupid blocks of flesh".

After Donna had somehow dodged the attack, she pulled herself together and started to think her way out of this situation. This process only began when she regained Cujo's intelligibility. She realized that it is rabid by looking at it in the eyes. Its eyes seemed to tell her some meaningful, intelligent message. They said, "I’m going to pull you to pieces, they said. You and the boy both. Just as soon as I find a way to get into this tin can, I’ll eat you alive; I’ll be swallowing pieces of you while you’re still screaming." (p.209)

It cannot be true that the eyes of Cujo themselves told those words to Donna. Nonetheless, Donna acquires some sort of understanding (pehaps misunderstanding) on Cujo. I believe Donna somehow managed to put an end to her ignorance at this moment, and that is why she could think her way out of this mess since then.
