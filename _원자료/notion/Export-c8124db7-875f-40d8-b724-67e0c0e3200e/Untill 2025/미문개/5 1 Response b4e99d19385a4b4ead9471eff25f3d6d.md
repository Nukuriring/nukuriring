The first chapter of “The Great Gatsby”, sets the stage for the story with a vivid depiction of the setting and the characters that inhabit it. The chapter describes the geography of the fictional town of West Egg, which is home to the enigmatic millionaire Jay Gatsby. It also introduces the narrator, Nick Carraway, and his cousin Daisy Buchanan, who lives across the bay in the more affluent East Egg.

Baz Luhrmann's 2013 film adaptation of The Great Gatsby takes a more visually spectacular approach to the story, with bright colors, elaborate costumes, and lavish sets. The movie clips captured this flamboyant aesthetic, with sweeping camera movements and stylized visual effects. movie uses green light for screen transitions, and it shows that Gatsby is occupying Carraway’s memory. In the novel, it is not clear whether Caraway has mental issues, but in the movie, it is clear that he does. This is revealed through his unshaven beard, facial expressions, and tone of voice. In addition, the movie does not only reflect the world from the first-person perspective, unlike the novel. The use of various music such as hip-hop, jazz, and classical as background music also shows that the movie is reacting to the world differently from the novel. Carraway's narration only describes the surface of things. In the movie, his reactions are shown through his face and expressions.

Unlike the novel, The movie conveys information to the audience that is not shared with the characters. When Tom Buchanan receives a call and enters the mansion, he leaves a brief message not to call back. Although this fact is not known to any of the characters, the audience can see this scene and confirm that Tom has a secret. Through direction, the movie creates a gap between the information that is told and shown, and that which is shared or hidden among the characters, making the audience constantly aware of it.

Fianlly, The movie's depiction of New York as a city of desire and ambition clearly shows that this story is about the era. By combining short videos from various media to show the appearance of New York, the movie presents an objective background that separates it from the personal opinion of a specific character and reveals the spirit of the times.
