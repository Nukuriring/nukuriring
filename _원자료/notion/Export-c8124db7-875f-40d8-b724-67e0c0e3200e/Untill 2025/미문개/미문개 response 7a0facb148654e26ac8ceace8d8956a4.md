Once the male characters have entered, Mrs. Hale and Mrs. Peters appear on the stage. However, in the adaptation, Mr. and Mrs. Hale enter the room first, followed by Mr. and Mrs. Peters. Finally, Mr. Henderson walks into the room. The order of entrance is not the only difference.
